# Combat update generation prompts

All three assets used the built-in `image_gen` tool with `transparent_background: true`. The two existing packed enemy sheets served as style and layout references. Sources, packed assets and reproduction instructions are in [README.md](README.md).

## Rigging Castaway

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production game sprite sheet for Molotology Evolution: a UNIQUE RIGGING CASTAWAY lost diver, a mutated deep sea diver distinct from the reference handcrab. Reference image establishes detailed gritty pixel art, 2-row 6-column layout and left-facing side view. Subject: tarnished tall brass diving helmet with a cracked vertical cyan visor, shredded dirty teal diving canvas, a broken cable spool on back instead of a coral carapace, one long barnacle-encrusted asymmetric crab claw, pale human rear hand, narrow crustacean legs. Clearly new silhouette; coherent same individual throughout. EXACTLY twelve full-body sprites in evenly spaced six columns and two rows. Row 1 six walking cycle poses; row 2 six attack poses: crouch anticipation, leap windup, airborne leap, extended claw lunge, landing, recovery. Every sprite faces left, same scale, fits fully in own cell, centered with ample transparent gutters, no detached effects or text. Match reference's restrained coral/brass/teal palette and sharp detailed pixel edges. Transparent background. Output wide 3:1 sheet.

## Cinder Diver

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game spritesheet for Molotology Evolution. Reference is existing hermit monster for style/layout only. Create a distinct CINDER DIVER: mutated lost deep-sea diver inhabiting a broken angular iron furnace helmet and black smoker chimney-shell, jagged charred plates with glowing orange seams, ash-white barnacles, two asymmetrical rusty red crab claws, pale vestigial human arms and small crustacean legs. Clearly different silhouette from the rounded spiral shell in reference, but same gritty detailed sharp pixel art. Exactly twelve separated full body left-facing sprites in 2 rows of 6 evenly spaced cells. Row1 six walk poses, row2 crouch windup, rising leap, airborne leap, fully extended lunge, heavy landing, recovery. Consistent character proportions and common scale across all poses. Ample transparent gutters, each creature wholly inside its cell. No text no detached effects no scenery. True transparent background. Wide 3:1 image.

## Molt relic

Use case: stylized-concept. A single collectible sprite for the dark underwater pixel-art game Molotology Evolution. The Molt relic: a naturally shed, split-open crustacean carapace, coral-red and tarnished dark brass outer plates parting to reveal a luminous pale teal pearl-like organic core, small curled shell fragments attached at its base. Recognizably an empty molted crab shell rather than a coin, gem, helmet or living animal. Detailed gritty sharp pixel art, restrained dirty teal/coral/brass palette, clear strong silhouette readable at 65x65 gameplay pixels. One centered compact object, three-quarter front view, no text no scenery no frame no cast background shadow; generous transparent padding. Truly transparent background.
